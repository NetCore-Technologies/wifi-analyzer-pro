What you can see

From radio noise to useful signal.

A practical toolkit for wireless engineers, help desks, MSPs and curious network owners. Actual capabilities depend on the adapter, driver and operating system.

⌁

Access point discovery

Inspect visible SSIDs, BSSIDs, channel, band, signal and advertised security from the host operating system.

▥

Channel intelligence

Compare nearby networks and identify overlapping 2.4 GHz channels plus distinct 5 GHz and 6 GHz channel occupancy.

◉

Signal diagnostics

Follow measured RSSI and available noise, link and interface details without pretending unsupported metrics are real.

⌖

Network health

Check gateway reachability, latency, DNS resolution and packet loss to focus troubleshooting.

▦

Device visibility

Review locally visible neighbour and ARP information, with vendor hints where the OUI is known.

⇅

Performance testing

Run throughput tests and export scan snapshots for reports, baselines and repeatable investigations.

Built for real environments

Powerful on the bench. Useful in production.

Designed to run close to the network being diagnosed: a laptop, engineering workstation or small Linux host such as a Raspberry Pi. No mandatory cloud account, no telemetry upload requirement and no dependency on a remote control plane.

For teams and enterprises

Visibility that respects your boundaries.

Self-host the tool, review the source and integrate it into your own operational practices. The current API does not provide authentication or enterprise identity controls. Before production deployment, keep it on loopback or place it behind your organisation's authenticated network boundary. SSO, RBAC, fleet management and signed update channels are future enterprise work—not capabilities claimed to exist today.
